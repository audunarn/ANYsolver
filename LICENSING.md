source licences, and third-party material retain their respective licences and

optional dependencies are listed in `dependency-licenses.json` and summarized

licences. In particular, the current extracted ANY ecosystem dependencies are
still GPL-3.0-or-later releases. Relicensing ANYsolver alone does not relicense
those packages or by itself remove obligations arising from distributing a
larger combined product.

author audit showing only owner-controlled identities plus local release and
