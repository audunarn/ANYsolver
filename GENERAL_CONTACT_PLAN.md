## 4. D2 first slice — partial overlap and boundaries

After D1 acceptance, or earlier following an explicit user decision, select one primary method from the authorized options. Compare their mechanics and integration cost on paper and with a bounded discriminator if necessary, obtain consequential design scrutiny, then implement an integrated case. Do not fully develop all options before selecting one.

- **Pressure agglomeration:** constrain or combine small-cut Q1 hats using admissible neighbouring functions. This changes the multiplier space and enforces aggregate weak constraints; the old individual constraints are not all retained. Derive nonnegative physical pressure, support/partition consistency, moment/work reproduction and transfer as the front moves. Independent gap checks must detect penetration that aggregation could conceal.
- **Dual/biorthogonal mortar with boundary modification:** derive the multiplier space and treatment of physical and prescribed boundaries. Nonnegative dual coefficients do not automatically imply nonnegative pointwise traction. State and verify the chosen admissible pressure cone and the effect on enforcement, work and positivity; naming a dual basis does not resolve small-cut stability.
- **Finite penalty-regularized weak-gap law:** explicitly relax exact complementarity with a registered penalty and physical-gap tolerance. Demonstrate response, conditioning and gap sensitivity. An augmented variant that converges back to the unchanged unstable exact law is not a repair of that law. Finite regularization is not evidence of inf-sup stability of the original multiplier pairing.

The first two options change the independent pressure space; the third changes exact active-gap enforcement. Each therefore changes a premise of the retained exact-law result, but that alone establishes no acceptable method. Use method-appropriate stability/consistency criteria plus the common integrated-response, nonnegative physical pressure, work and dimensional-gap requirements. Preserve the cut-front and prescribed-edge constructions as discriminators. Qualify moving fronts, opening/recontact, physical source boundaries, state transfer and restart; neither a constant-traction patch nor one cut family is sufficient by itself.

This authority permits a changed pressure basis near cut fronts. An enriched physical-subscale/E4-PL shell remains a separate user decision because it changes the structural/contact profile. Escalate early if the authorized options are not credible; it is unnecessary to implement and fail all three merely to request that decision.

Complete the remaining D2 shell combinations, finite rotation, warped geometry, physical boundaries, rigid shapes, general search/swept control, automatic pairing and self-contact under their own admitted rows. Update this goal/plan pair with a bounded D2 delivery objective at that transition.
