## Proof contract

Let B=P.T A_rep P - sigma I be the exact-real shifted stored representative under a complete symmetric permutation P, with the same fixed sigma=2*derived epsilon. Choose a finite nonsingular exact stored binary64 lower-triangular F. Backend L and positive diagU may construct this candidate, but are not proof evidence by themselves. Regard the final rounded F as exact; no separate square-root or column-scaling construction error is needed, because the independently enclosed B-F F.T residual covers their effect.

For D=diag(abs(Fii)) and N=abs(strictLower(F)), M=D-N is an invertible triangular comparison matrix. Since D^-1 N is strictly lower triangular, its inverse series is finite and nonnegative; entrywise abs(F^-1) <= M^-1. Thus forward y=M^-1 1 gives ||F^-1||inf <= max(y), and backward z=M^-T 1 gives ||F^-1||1 <= max(z). These are O(nnz(F)) recurrences and require no global inverse.

With outward upper bounds binf,b1, form product=up(b1*binf), then mu=down(1/product). This certifies lambda_min(F F.T) >= mu. Independently enclose rho >= ||B-F F.T||2. If a positive downward-rounded mu-rho is obtained, B is SPD. With the unchanged mechanical error epsilon and graph-metric bound G, the old conservative down((sigma-epsilon)/G) transfer remains valid; a separately directed additional factor margin could only strengthen it. No complete mixed-K SPD or unscaled-coordinate eigenvalue claim follows.

The comparison inverse theorem does not apply to a matrix with upper entries silently ignored. Complete symmetric permutation and the original representative/shift hashes must be bound. A backend row permutation or non-LDL U is harmless only because its resulting candidate is verified against this actual B; it cannot justify treating diagU alone as inertia evidence.

## Counterexamples and likely limitations

A lower-triangular all-ones F has actual inverse equal to the first-difference bidiagonal matrix; its inverse1 and infinity norms are at most 2, so lambda_min(F F.T) >= 1/4. The comparison recurrence instead gives its last component 2^(n-1), making the proposed bound exponentially small and eventually overflowing. This is a valid HOLD despite a positive well-behaved inverse. Larger precision alone cannot remove that comparison pessimism.

Exact triangularity is consequential. If the recurrence ignored the upper entry in F=[[1,2],[0,1]], it would incorrectly claim mu=1; actual F F.T=[[5,2],[2,1]] has minimum eigenvalue3-sqrt8, below 0.3. Taking B=F F.T-0.3I yields rho=0.3 but indefinite B, so the incorrect recurrence would falsely certify it. The proposed construction is lower triangular, but its input validation must enforce that property.

Residual and inverse majorants may both be conservative; failure of mu>rho proves neither indefinite mechanics nor inadequate formulation. Backend nonpositive D, overflow, underflow refusal or resource refusal is also HOLD only.

## Resources and discriminating controls

The factor need not be dense, but F F.T can acquire the union of column-support cliques and can be nearly dense even when many exact contributions later cancel. Before native sparse multiplication, bound symbolic output/work from complete supports, and account for F, both signed and absolute products, Bhat, residual/difference temporaries, permutation copies and recurrence arrays together. The existing product helper allocates both products before returning and does not impose its own sparse output budget. Do not silently widen existing admissions or claim global memory from the interval pattern estimate. Keep one supervised job and unchanged 600s/1GiB/8-thread cap; a separate explicitly bounded same-case residual implementation may refuse rather than allocate an excessive global product. Row/block streaming residual verification is an alternative if full product structure exceeds the cap, but needs its own rounding/work contract.

Small exact Fraction controls should verify both recurrence inequalities for signed lower factors, nonunit diagonals, transpose asymmetry, cancellation and subnormal products; reject missing diagonal/upper-entry/duplicate input. Independently test exact Bhat shift and F F.T residual containment, strict-positive versus zero/negative margin, symmetric permutation mismatch and a bad backend-derived factor. Include the all-ones comparison-growth control so HOLD is not mislabeled failure. No such implementation or runtime has been assessed here.
