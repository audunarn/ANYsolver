# Supporting fixed 18 block-left design review V1

Read-only mathematical review; no numerical work, test, source edit, Git operation or worker. Same stored F and shifted representative as V2; default scalar route and historical evidence must remain unchanged. No qualification/F03 acceptance.

Verdict: the proposed theorem is valid, with the directed-operation and validation conditions below. It does not guarantee the selected fixed 18 blocks will yield a useful enclosure.

Let W be any exact stored square matrix and Ghat the exact stored rounded product of W F, with ||WF-Ghat||2<=eg. Suppose the validated lower-triangular Ghat comparison recurrences establish ||Ghat^-1||2<=b. If eta>=b*eg and eta<1, then WF=Ghat(I+Ghat^-1 E) is invertible by Neumann, and ||(WF)^-1||2<=b/(1-eta). Hence F^-1=(WF)^-1 W and ||F^-1||2<=b||W||2/(1-eta). The resulting reciprocal squared upper bound gives muF for the original F F.T. W is a proof preconditioner, not a physical connection law or work chart change.

Local inverse construction rounding needs no extra certification error because W is treated as exact stored. Even a poor approximate W is mathematically permissible: the complete WF product error, Ghat inverse bound and Neumann condition must independently certify it. A positive Neumann condition also guarantees actual W invertibility; do not substitute a backend/local-pivot verdict for those bounds. Only local inversions<=18 are permitted in construction, with the fixed row partition declared before evaluating margins. No global inverse or block-size sweep.

Essential arithmetic:

- Use upward b=sqrt(up(inv1*invinf)), with the existing checked square-root enclosure, and upward induced2 majorant w>=||W||2 from complete directed sparse row/column sums.
- Enclose the entire exact WF product using guarded sparse_product; its Frobenius error safely majorizes induced2. Validate Ghat finite canonical exact lower triangular with all nonzero diagonals before comparison. 'Strictly lower' would have zero diagonal and be singular; the requirement is lower triangular with invertible diagonal.
- eta=up(b*eg); denominator=down(1-eta)>0. Enclose numerator products and final division upward to obtain h>=b*w/(1-eta). Square h upward and take reciprocal downward. Any overflow, underflow-domain refusal, nonpositive denominator/mu or final muF-rho not strictly positive is HOLD. Using an upward denominator, downward product or rounded-to-zero eta can invalidate the proof.
