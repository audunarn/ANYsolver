# GE-B3 G3c proof-compressed completion successor

Status: IMPLEMENTATION_CANDIDATE.  This successor begins at accepted timing
gate `a0b7e931`.  It preserves the original 375 histories, 3,075 accepted
events, 3,450 restart prefixes and 3,825 ordered assignments.  It changes only
how repeated covariance-equivalent cases are proved; it does not change an
operator, case, tolerance, restart rule, public selector or production default.

The unoptimized exhaustive campaign was independently estimated at 88--144
hours for two cycles.  That execution layout is rejected as an engineering
process, not reclassified as scientific evidence.

## Executed basis

Each cycle executes exactly 25 genuine histories:

* 15 roots: every graph, `BASE`, `NONE`, at force scales S0, S1 and S2;
* five reversal/common-motion holdouts: every graph,
  `CONNECTIVITY_REVERSED`, S1, `CM2`;
* five proper-global/common-motion holdouts: every graph,
  `PROPER_GLOBAL_TRANSFORM`, S2, `CM3`.

Every executed history writes every prefix packet.  Exactly 120 genuine fresh
restart continuations execute: all six prefixes of each root and prefixes zero,
midpoint and final for each holdout.  The final prefix is deliberately included
as a constructor/import and exact-byte identity check.

## Derived closure

An independently authored standard-library checker reconstructs the complete
cross-product from literals, not from the producer owner.  Every nonexecuted
case is associated with one same-graph, same-scale BASE/NONE root and a frozen
composition of numbering transport and common-motion covariance.  The two
nontrivial holdouts per graph test reversal plus CM2 and proper-global plus CM3.
The already accepted local 25-variant owner inventory and corrected rehearsal
remain immutable prerequisites for shuffled insertion, renumbering, reversal,
proper-global transport, CM0--CM3, work, recovery and restart identities.

The checker rejects missing/duplicate cases, unknown transforms, changed case
commands, scale substitution, graph substitution, incomplete prefix coverage,
unbound prerequisite hashes, mutation of any derivation edge, or provenance
other than `EXECUTED` and `DERIVED_BY_VERIFIED_TRANSPORT`.  It emits all 375
history dispositions and all 3,825 original assignment dispositions in their
original order.  Derived entries bind the executed root, applicable holdout,
transport proof and original assignment; they are never represented as an
executed solve.

This proof is valid only for the registered private, elastic, affine G3c graph
domain.  It does not extrapolate to generic geometry or history-bearing
materials.

## Bounded execution

At most three workers run concurrently.  Each worker uses one numerical-library
thread, at most 24 GiB, a 600-second child limit and a 120-second inactivity
watchdog.  Each wave is at most 1,800 seconds.  No automatic retry or authority
reuse is permitted.  A projected wave above 25 minutes must be split or further
optimized before dispatch; time is a process safeguard, not scientific
acceptance.

Two fresh cycles are required.  Their canonical scientific aggregates must be
byte-identical.  Timings, paths, PIDs and raw process measurements are external.
An independent checker must not import the production owner, its comparison
helpers, cached matrices or production recovery.
