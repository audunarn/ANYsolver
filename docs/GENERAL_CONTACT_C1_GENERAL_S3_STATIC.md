# General nodal S3/S3 static contact development profile

Two distinct development profiles connect independently moving S3 V2D physical skins to the ordinary nonlinear static solver: `S3_S3_GENERAL_NODAL_POINTWISE_PENALTY_STATIC_DEV_V0` and `S3_S3_GENERAL_NODAL_PRIMAL_MORTAR_PENALTY_STATIC_DEV_V0`. Each corner may move independently, including the position change caused by small rotations of an offset skin. Both profiles are opt-in development routes. The mortar route begins the programme's selected P1 pressure-space formulation, but projected augmentation, pressure-space qualification and C1 engineering acceptance remain open.

The interaction names one S3 integration face and one S3 target face. Each face has a declared `top`, `bottom` or `midsurface` side. Both use an exact S3 V2D element, isotropic elastic material and disjoint material nodes. The fixed reference source normal must point from target to source, and selected physical sides must face each other. `pair_motion="general"`, a selected `normal_discretization`, explicitly dimensioned `normal_stiffness` (Pa/m), `gap_tolerance` (m) and `general_s3_difference_step` (m) are required. The differentiation and swept subdivision budgets are finite settings. Use `kinematics="von_karman"` in `solve_static_nonlinear`.

The current source triangle supplies the oriented contact plane. Both laws integrate over source **reference area**. The pointwise law clips projected overlap by the affine negative-gap region and integrates squared gap. The mortar law retains exactly three nonnegative P1 pressure basis functions on the source triangle. It exactly integrates their reference-area support `w_i` and weighted gap `b_i` over moving overlap, then uses `g_i=b_i/w_i` and `p_i=epsilon_n max(-g_i,0)` in `0.5 epsilon_n sum_i w_i min(g_i,0)^2`. Overlap clipping never adds pressure DOFs. `normalized_weighted_gap_m`, `numerical_pressure_pa` and `reference_support_m2` are reported separately from the independent physical gap. A weak normalized gap may be open even where the physical gap is negative; the independent gap check remains mandatory.

Local gradients and Hessians are bounded numerical derivatives of the selected scalar energy at smooth branches, then pulled back through the formulation-owned skin position map. Both faces receive force and moment. At opening or exact zero energy, the manager returns the zero generalized derivative. Projection, overlap and activation derivative kinks are detected by the kernels and fail explicitly when a smooth Hessian cannot be established.

The reported `minimum_gap` is a conservative lower bound over all nine physical corner pairs along the current source normal. It may be more negative than the true gap in the projected overlap, especially on tilted or partial-overlap configurations. `sampled_overlap_gap` is the separately evaluated minimum affine gap at the overlap polygon vertices. The swept trial check bounds every vertex-pair signed gap along the **continuous linearized skin path**. It uses quadratic Bézier controls for the changing source and target normals; unresolved tilt, polarity or gap causes cutback or failure. This bound can reject safe steps because it covers all corner pairs, including pairs outside projected overlap. It does not certify finite-rotation director paths.

The example [general_contact_s3_nodal_pair.py](../examples/general_contact_s3_nodal_pair.py) selects the mortar penalty route with prescribed displacement, physical bottom/top skins, finite overlap, recovered reactions, three numerical pressure coefficients and a version 2 restart checkpoint. Its 20 mm gap tolerance is deliberately permissive for an integrated development smoke case; it is not an engineering penetration recommendation. Focused checks exercise nonuniform nodal motion, balanced global wrench, initial penetration rejection, swept cutback and deterministic continuation from restart for both discretizations. The S3 mortar scalar checks independently cover constant traction, positive full-overlap pressure rank, partial-overlap reference weights, in-plane stretch and smooth virtual work/tangent.

Remaining work includes Q4/S3 mortar contact, projected augmentation, pressure-space stability and partial-contact qualification, tighter gap and enforcement convergence, general crossing ownership and finite sliding, nonmatching multi-face patches, objective friction history, independent review and installed-package acceptance. Neither development profile establishes the programme's first usable paired-shell handoff.
