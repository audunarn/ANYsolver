# General nodal S3/S3 static contact development profile

`S3_S3_GENERAL_NODAL_POINTWISE_PENALTY_STATIC_DEV_V0` connects two S3 V2D physical skins to the ordinary nonlinear static solver. It permits each corner to move independently, including the position change caused by small rotations of an offset skin. It is an opt-in development profile. The programme's selected shell law remains the primal-pressure mortar formulation; this pointwise penalty profile does not establish mortar pressure-space stability or C1 engineering acceptance.

The interaction names one S3 integration face and one S3 target face. Each face has a declared `top`, `bottom` or `midsurface` side. Both use an exact S3 V2D element, isotropic elastic material and disjoint material nodes. The fixed reference source normal must point from target to source, and selected physical sides must face each other. `pair_motion="general"`, `normal_discretization="pointwise_penalty"`, an explicitly dimensioned `normal_stiffness` (Pa/m), `gap_tolerance` (m) and `general_s3_difference_step` (m) are required. The finite differentiation and swept subdivision budgets are explicit settings. Use `kinematics="von_karman"` in `solve_static_nonlinear`.

The current source triangle supplies the oriented contact plane. Its projected overlap with the target is clipped by the affine negative-gap region. The penalty energy integrates over source **reference area**. Local gradients and Hessians are numerical derivatives of that scalar energy at smooth branches, then pulled back through the formulation-owned skin position map. Both faces receive force and moment. At opening or exact zero energy, the manager returns the zero generalized derivative. Projection, overlap and activation derivative kinks are detected by the local kernel and fail explicitly when a smooth Hessian cannot be established.

The reported `minimum_gap` is a conservative lower bound over all nine physical corner pairs along the current source normal. It may be more negative than the true gap in the projected overlap, especially on tilted or partial-overlap configurations. `sampled_overlap_gap` is the separately evaluated minimum affine gap at the overlap polygon vertices. The swept trial check bounds every vertex-pair signed gap along the **continuous linearized skin path**. It uses quadratic Bézier controls for the changing source and target normals; unresolved tilt, polarity or gap causes cutback or failure. This bound can reject safe steps because it covers all corner pairs, including pairs outside projected overlap. It does not certify finite-rotation director paths.

The example [general_contact_s3_nodal_pair.py](../examples/general_contact_s3_nodal_pair.py) uses prescribed displacement, physical bottom/top skins, finite overlap, recovered reactions and a version 2 restart checkpoint. Its 20 mm gap tolerance is deliberately permissive for an integrated development smoke case; it is not an engineering penetration recommendation. Focused checks exercise nonuniform nodal motion, balanced global wrench, initial penetration rejection, swept cutback and deterministic continuation from restart. The previous S3 energy and skin-map checks provide independent local virtual-work and tangent evidence.

Remaining work includes selected Q4/S3 mortar contact, pressure-space stability, tighter gap and enforcement convergence, general crossing ownership and finite sliding, nonmatching multi-face patches, objective friction history, independent review and installed-package acceptance. This profile must not be advertised as the programme's first usable paired-shell handoff.
