## Full coverage, fixed before observing results

The original five graphs and five variants remain unchanged. For every ordered
graph/variant pair, use force scales 0.01, 1.0 and 10.0 and motions NONE, CM0,
CM1, CM2 and CM3, in that order. This is 375 distinct history probes within
25 graph-variant records, not 375 new geometries. The JSON binds every probe

is deterministic and hash-bound, including prefix zero and the final prefix.

NONE has five LOAD_STAGE commands. Each CM probe starts from a genuine virgin
owner, accepts its four preparation commands, then the same five load stages.
This gives 3075 accepted-stage events and 3450 restart-prefix probes per full
cycle. Each force scale starts a fresh history; no previous scale is carried
over. All 375 probes and all prefixes remain required, even if a shorter smoke
test passes. No sample, rejected case or unexecuted case is positive coverage.

Load factors, root order, physical port frames, offsets, shell normals, sections,
numbering maps, thresholds and all three directional h values remain exactly
the inherited fixture/owner contracts. CM3 remains a 1.4*pi common rotation;

an accepted owner, imported material authority or numerical result to stand

No arbitrary increase in bounds or omission of cases after observing timings.

## Numbering, common motion and exact serialization

Renumber by the existing exact node/element/joint maps and inverse-map before
comparison. Connectivity reversal preserves physical anchors, directors and
orientation authority. Compare assembled physical quantities; local material
and internal bases require their explicitly derived transport, not raw matrix
component equality. Passive coordinate re-expression uses x'=U*x+b,
Q'=U*Q*U^T and the inherited U/b. Active motion keeps the reference fixed and
uses x*=W*x+t, Q*=W*Q. Under both actions, nodal virtual translation/spatial
rotation coordinates transform by the action's 3x3 rotation.

Constraint multipliers are dual to their actual constraint components. Under
passive re-expression, support translation AND support rotation rows transform
by U; material port/joint rows remain invariant. Under active common motion,
support translation rows transform by W, support rotation rows and material
joint rows remain invariant. Use these block maps for residual, tangent,
reaction, direction and virtual-work checks. For combined passive/common
motion use W'=U*W*U^T and t'=U*t+b-W'*b at every preparation step. Do not
compare accumulated rotational bookkeeping as physical orientation.

SHUFFLED_INSERTION must produce identical canonical expanded construction and
identical complete physical payloads. Checkpoints carry distinct case-selector
provenance, so raw outer checkpoint bytes are not claimed identical across
different selectors. The frozen comparison projection retains state schema,
epoch, total_u, Q, multipliers, all native payloads and all adapter fields except
the graph-origin/previous-envelope hash links. Independently verify those links
in each complete unprojected checkpoint. The removed fields are exactly state
definition_sha256/previous_sha256 and adapter origin_sha256/previous_sha256;
no other data or floating spelling is normalized. The canonical comparison
projection is diagnostic only, never restart authority. Identical selector and
command history replay MUST match the entire unprojected checkpoint bytes.
