defensible, refinement-stable work transfer. The saved Q4 Schur matrices show

## Ordered work before another scientific registration

1. **Supported energy.** Derive the constrained elastic energy structure of
   both classical Q4 and CST-CB diagnostic operators. Establish an
   independently validated lower-energy test or inertia/smallest-algebraic
   check that can reveal negative modes away from zero. Validate on bounded
   intentionally indefinite and nearly singular examples, plus the current
   small positive dense oracle. An inverse shift probe by itself is
   insufficient. Report conditioning and residual-based error limits, or
   continue labeling the resulting Schur diagnostic provisional.
2. **Residual algorithm.** Diagnose why the CST-CB nearest-zero eigenpair has
   a `1.0129580633e-8` residual under a `1e-8` limit. Investigate solver
   stopping tolerance, scale, repeatability and residual definition on new
   bounded fixtures. A prospective algorithm change may request a tighter
   eigensolver tolerance, but the consumed result cannot be accepted or
   rerun retrospectively.
3. **Interface modes.** Use the saved Q4 matrices for read-only modal
   localization, eigencluster/subspace, physical virtual-work and mass/H1
   content analysis. Then construct bounded independent patch problems that
   vary the shell host, beam trace and multiplier resolution one at a time.
   Reproduce the diagnostic mode with both Q4 and CST-CB before attributing
   the decline to P1. Do not count post-hoc choices as preregistered proof.
4. **Candidate spaces.** Derive and compare the G0-required piecewise-constant
   space, continuous linear P1 and a justified dual-space candidate where
   possible. Freeze interpolation, quadrature, segmentation and physical
   displacement/multiplier norms. Test patch work, row stability, gaps and
   refinement over 1:1, 1:2, 1:4, 1:8 and reciprocal mesh ratios. Keep a
   separate diagnostic penalty comparator; it cannot substitute for exact
   multiplier production mechanics.
5. **Physical criterion.** Establish a lower bound/trend criterion from
   supported physical patch and resolved-interface cases, with unit-invariant
   normalization and absolute floors. Register systematic refinement and a
   holdout, with independent mechanics review before any new full-size run.
   Do not set a threshold from the observed Q4 values alone.
