# F03 next physical reference: independent variational line construction

22 September 2026. **Design work before registration or scientific execution.** The earlier 96-case classical comparison and 12-case CalculiX B31/CPS screen passed their respective discrete equilibrium checks but did not converge to a physical target. The signed-graph KKT v1 diagnostic likewise does not supply a loaded reference. This design advances the mandatory G0B reference; it does not select K4/local-dual or qualify a native B3-GE/E4-PL line law.

## Mechanical model to derive and preflight

Build an independently implemented, small-strain plane-stress/Timoshenko variational comparator, without importing the native shell/beam stiffness, trace, coupling or load operators. Use a panel `[-.5,1.5]×[-.5,.5] m`, thickness `.07 m`, `E=210 GPa`, `ν=.3`, fixed outer x edges, independently assembled Q4 and CST meshes. Freeze the CST diagonal/orientation and split every shell-sample host crossing explicitly. Use a one-metre rooted beam with `A=.01 m²`, `As=.008 m²`, `I=8.333333333333333e−6 m⁴`, and `G=E/[2(1+ν)]`; fix its root pose, leave its tip free. Define four material surface samples at offsets `(±.2,±.23) m`, centered shell centroid and proper-fit in-plane yaw with conversion length `.46 m`. Make shell traces and beam kinematics separately testable under rigid motions and affine strain.

Use a conforming independent beam trial with enough resolution of bending and distributed load, e.g. cubic-Hermite `v`, independent quadratic `θ`, and quadratic axial `u`, with the *same trial fields* in internal energy, external loading and interface virtual work. Specify continuity and exact polynomial quadrature orders before construction; register an independent high-order quadrature convergence tolerance for the trigonometric load terms. Do not graft a load particular onto only one of those operations. Use `q_y(s)=10^6[1+.25 cos(2πs)]` and `10^6 sin(2πs) N/m` as separate signed physical histories. Integrate the line gap against independently implemented P0 and continuous P1 mortar spaces on an exact shell/beam/mortar overlay, with exact multiplier work and a consistent symmetric static tangent. A spring, point-tie chain or strong coefficient equality is only a separately labeled comparator.

The **provisional endpoint model** imposes two direct translational gap equations at `s=0` (the beam root translation is fixed, the shell root trace is physically free); yaw is weakly bonded on the open line, with no additional root yaw or tip point equation. This is not yet an accepted endpoint law. A point evaluation on a two-dimensional continuum shell is not automatically a bounded functional in the H¹ energy norm; a finite-mesh independent row can still create a mesh-dependent point restraint. Derive its virtual work, prove that the point rows are independent of supports and mortar rows on each admitted mesh, verify stress-free reference and patch responses, and demonstrate a mesh-stable physical endpoint meaning without locking. If that boundedness or the other checks fail, revise to a derived integrated endpoint/footprint law or the pure weak-line continuum law *before* a qualification registration. Run a separate omission of just these two rows at the anchor mesh as sensitivity only if the point law passes preflight; it is a different law, not an interchangeable outcome.

The continuous gap cannot be inferred from weak residual or station samples. On every material overlay interval the shell trace is affine in `s`, while the declared beam fields are polynomial. Compute an upper enclosure for each absolute translation and `.46`-scaled yaw gap by checking interval endpoints and all real stationary roots of the polynomial gap and bounding floating-point/root error. Sum no arbitrary sample estimates into a purported certificate. Report the maximum over the entire bonded interval divided by the frozen displacement scale `U=10^6/(210e9·.07) m`. Include interface traction jumps, total force/moment and virtual-work cancellation.

## Proposed bounded refinement inventory

For each of Q4/CST and smooth/sine load, use four joint `(Ns,Ny,Nb,Nm)` levels `(16,8,64,8)`, `(32,16,128,16)`, `(64,32,256,32)`, `(128,64,512,64)`. At the anchor `(64,32,256,32)`, independently vary each axis to `Nm=16,32,64`, `Nb=128,256,512`, `Ns=32,64,128` at `Ny=32`, and `Ny=16,32,64` at `Ns=64`, holding other axes fixed. The anchor is already a joint level, so there are **12 distinct coupled mesh tuples per topology/load**, not 13: four joint tuples plus eight new one-axis tuples. This is `12×2×2=48` mesh/load/formulation combinations. Add the endpoint-omitted anchor for each topology/load (`+4`), giving **52 distinct combinations**. P0 and P1 are separate solves, **104 registered solves** if the full proposed inventory is frozen. The executable runner must derive and assert this exact count and reject duplicates.

Before that inventory can be frozen, run only small preflight derivation/unit cases for Q4/CST patch parity, beam analytical axial/bending/shear patches, four-sample pose/work, root-row independence, P0/P1 trace moment and quadrature parity, as well as an upper-bound method for the continuous gap. Fix normalization, row scales, response and energy absolute floors, mesh-resource estimates, source/dependency identities and the external-solver interpretation. Then independently review a source-frozen single-use supervisor with one-thread serial cases, exclusive root, sampled RSS ≤8 GiB, 900 s/case and 7,200 s total; these are resource limits, **not** evidence that the largest cases fit. Preserve partial and failed cases.

Record signed beam/shell station motion, tip response, shell/beam energy, load and interface work, support force and moment, weak compatibility, multiplier rank and norm, certified continuous gap, runtime, memory and failed work. For any numerical balance >`1e−8`, rank loss, or resource limit, the physical reference fails. A provisional convergence screen requires independent terminal Nm32→64, Nb256→512, Ns64→128 and Ny32→64 changes ≤`1%`, plus P0/P1 and Q4/CST agreement ≤`1%` for registered physical responses and energies with nonzero case-specific floors fixed before execution, and a certified gap ≤`.05 U`. Show at least three systematic joint levels; four are proposed. A failed comparison remains failed evidence, and no candidate may be chosen because one load or one discretization happens to agree.

Passing would establish at most a matched **classical planar continuum comparator**, with documented beam/shell formulation differences. It would not turn it into an exact native B3-GE/E4-PL discrete oracle, qualify eccentric six-channel torque or transverse-shear transfer, prove uniform signed KKT stability, or close F03/G0B. The required executed external references remain separate G0/G6 obligations; the prior B31/CPS and mixed-U1 evidence retains its adverse limits.
