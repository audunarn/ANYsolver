transport/recovery, Q1Y3 local-algebra, Q1Z support, and Q1Z2 completion

hashes, schemas, coverage, terminal predicates, and cross-layer identities.

Two fresh synthesis outputs must be byte-identical before the evidence record
is frozen.

An independent five-key review is required before final status. Successful
synthesis and review authorize only preparation of a separate Q1B plan.

and API/default changes remain unauthorized.
