# F03 proposed loaded classical-reference acceptance contract

22 September 2026. **Draft for independent mechanics review, before registration or loaded inventory execution.** This concerns only the independently implemented, small-strain plane-stress/Timoshenko variational comparator in `B3_GE_E4_PL_G0_F03_INDEPENDENT_LOADED_REFERENCE_DESIGN.md`. It cannot select the native B3-GE/E4-PL law, qualify eccentric six-channel torque, close F03/G0B, or replace mandatory executed external-solver references.

## Fixed physical scales and measures

The panel dimensions are 2 m in x and 1 m in y; the bonded line has `L0=1 m`. Shell `E=210 GPa`, thickness `t=.07 m`; reference width `W0=1 m`; beam properties and both signed load histories are those already declared. Let `q0=10^6 N/m`, `F0=q0 L0=10^6 N`, `M0=F0 L0=10^6 N m`, `K0=E t W0/L0=14.7×10^9 N/m`, and `U0=F0/K0=6.802721088435374×10^-5 m`. `U0` is the **declared shell membrane displacement scale** for the load resultant, independent of every computed response and candidate. It is not an estimate or lower bound for the coupled displacement. Keep the shell-pose yaw conversion `Lc=.46 m`; translations and `Lc×yaw` all have displacement units. Let `E0=F0 U0≈68.02721088435374 J` and line length `L0` be fixed across every mesh and law.

For the continuous finite-element fields, define the **primary compatibility observable**

\[
G_2=\frac{1}{U_0\sqrt{L_0}}
\left[\int_0^{L_0}
\bigl(g_x^2+g_y^2+(L_c g_\theta)^2\bigr)\,ds\right]^{1/2},
\qquad g=p_b-p_s.
\]

The weak mortar rows `Cq` are separately required to satisfy a scaled equation residual, but zero weak rows cannot substitute for `G2`. Report the exact-rational reconstructed-field lower/upper enclosure of each component's global finite-mesh pointwise maximum, plus endpoint and fixed-physical-band interior maxima. These are diagnostics, **not** a mesh-uniform H¹ point-evaluation acceptance threshold. Floating station evaluations may be compared with the rational field only with an explicit rounding/discretization interpretation; neither their sampled maximum nor the exact-field bound alone claims a bound on all rounded operations.

Proposed terminal acceptance: `G2≤0.01` for each topology, P0/P1 law and load history at the largest joint mesh. This is a **chosen finite-resolution engineering allowance**, corresponding to an RMS bond gap of `0.680272 µm`; it does not follow mathematically from `E` or `t` and does not establish a continuum exact bond. Let the anchor `A=(64,32,256,32)` and largest joint mesh `T=(128,64,512,64)`, ordered `(Ns,Ny,Nb,Nm)`. For each one-axis and joint pair named below require `G2_new≤max(1.05 G2_A,0.001)`; the fixed `0.001` normalized floor avoids a spurious relative-growth failure near numerical zero and is below the terminal `0.01` ceiling. Report all four joint levels and each one-axis value without suppressing nonmonotonicity. **Proposed additional continuum screen for review:** at fixed `(64,32,256)`, the mortar refinements `Nm=16→32→64` must each reduce `G2` to `≤max(0.8 G2_previous,0.001)`, with the `0.001` floor equal to `0.0680272 µm` RMS. This contraction is a preregistration numerical target, not an established convergence theorem. A plateau above that floor blocks a claim of mortar convergence even if the terminal finite-resolution allowance passes. Separately inspect all four joint levels for sustained decay; a finite set alone cannot prove a uniform inf-sup bound or continuum limit.

Also report the secondary, response-aware diagnostic `G_motion=||g||_L2/[max(U_motion,0.1 U0)√L0]`, with `|p|_Lc²=p_x²+p_y²+(Lc p_θ)²` and `U_motion=[(1/(2L0))∫₀ᴸ⁰(|pb|_Lc²+|ps|_Lc²)ds]¹ᐟ²`. Propose `G_motion≤0.05` at `T` in addition to the fixed-scale `G2≤0.01`; this chosen 5% relative-motion allowance is an engineering criterion, not a continuum theorem. The fixed primary gate prevents a large computed motion from hiding a large absolute gap. Review must assess the physical error budget before registration. A terminal value passing by scale alone cannot hide a lack of convergence: the signed physical responses and energies must independently satisfy the refinement checks below.

## Loaded response and balance inventory

The mandatory signed response vector is beam tip `(u,v,Lc θ)` and shell trace pose `(u,v,Lc θ)` at each material station `s=.25,.5,.75`, plus separate **nonnegative** shell and beam stored strain energies. Also record load work; support `Fx,Fy,Mz` **separately for the left shell x edge, right shell x edge, and beam root**, and their global sum about origin `(0,0)`; interface dual work, weak residual, `G2`, `G_motion`, multiplier norms, all three bounded pointwise maxima, equation residual, time/RSS and failed work. The shell edge moment is `Σ_nodes(x_i R_y,i−y_i R_x,i)` about `(0,0)`; the beam-root moment adds the physical lever arm of its support force and its constrained section-rotation generalized reaction. The Hermite slope row is free in this reference model and its residual belongs to the free-equilibrium check, not to a support reaction. Node-by-node reactions are not convergence comparators because node sets change with refinement. Keep smooth and sine histories separate and preserve signs. The only applied load in this classical case is the distributed beam force; its analytic total vertical resultant/moment are `(F0, F0 L0/2)` for smooth and `(0,-F0 L0/(2π))` for sine. With residual `Kq+Cᵀλ−f` and support reaction defined positive in that residual, require `|ΣRx|/F0≤1e−8`, `|ΣRy+Fy_applied|/F0≤1e−8`, and `|ΣMz+Mz_applied|/M0≤1e−8`. Stored energy must obey `2(Es+Eb)=external work` in this linear homogeneous model; interface work cancels between its two sides.

Scale each weak row by its mortar-test measure: `|∫φ_i g_channel ds|/[U0∫φ_i ds]≤1e−9` at accepted equilibrium, where the yaw channel is explicitly `g_channel=Lc gθ` and the other two are translational. This is an equation check and cannot replace `G2`. For free coordinate equilibrium, scale shell translations and beam axial/transverse displacement rows by `F0`; scale beam Hermite slope and section-rotation rows by `M0`; require the maximum scaled free residual `≤1e−8` with those fixed nonzero scales. Verify 16-point versus 24-point beam load quadrature to `≤1e−10 F0` for resultant and `≤1e−10 M0` for moment. On each exact overlay interval `g²` is a polynomial of degree at most six, so order-four Gauss integrates `G2` exactly for the floating polynomial representation; register order-six or eight parity and verify overlay completeness at the largest model. This is distinct from the exact-rational reconstructed-field pointwise bound.

For a matched signed response `a,b`, use `|a-b|/max(|a|,|b|,A_floor)`. Use floors `0.1 U0` for each displacement/converted yaw, `0.001 E0` for each separate stored energy, `0.01 F0` for grouped support force and `0.01 M0` for grouped support moment. Each comparison must be `≤1%`. If both compared values `a,b` of the **same stored-energy component** are below `0.001 E0`, classify that component as inactive and instead require `|a-b|≤10^-5 E0`; report both nonnegative energies and their signed difference `a−b`. Signed displacement/reaction components near zero are reported with signed differences and their explicit absolute floors, never suppressed.

The exact mandatory refinement pairs are `A→(64,32,256,64)` (mortar), `A→(64,32,512,32)` (beam), `A→(128,32,256,32)` (shell x), `A→(64,64,256,32)` (shell y), **and `A→T` (joint terminal)**. Require `≤1%` for every mandatory signed response/energy and grouped support reaction on each pair. At the terminal joint mesh, compare P0/P1 and Q4/CST under the same rule for each load. Report any quantity whose physical idealization or convergence branch makes the comparison inapplicable rather than omitting it; an inapplicable mandatory comparison blocks the classical reference pending revised preregistration. All four joint refinement levels remain mandatory. Balance and weak equation residuals are checked in addition to these comparisons.

## Review amendments proposed before registration

The `1%` signed-response comparison and the fixed floors are chosen Cauchy-screen allowances, not external accuracy validation. Their near-zero implications must be visible in adjudication: a displacement or converted yaw below `0.1U0` can differ by `0.001U0≈68.027 nm` at the comparison limit; a grouped force below `0.01F0` can differ by `100 N`; an inactive energy uses `10⁻⁵E0≈0.00068027 J`. The `0.001E0` energy activity floor and `0.01M0` moment floor are equally fixed screening choices. The exact physical error-budget rationale for these allowances remains pending; none may be described as a validated ideal bond simply because a finite-mesh screen passes.

At each load's terminal `T`, explicitly compare these four pairs: Q4 P0↔Q4 P1, CST P0↔CST P1, Q4 P0↔CST P0 and Q4 P1↔CST P1. Apply every pair to every beam-tip and shell-station signed channel, each separate stored energy, and each `(Fx,Fy,Mz)` of the left shell edge, right shell edge and beam root. Apply the same complete inventory of channels and reaction groups to every mandatory refinement pair. No omitted near-zero channel can be silently replaced by an aggregate norm.

Proposed additional numeric work checks for review: require `|2(Es+Eb)+λᵀCq−fᵀq|/E0≤1e−8` and `|λᵀCq|/E0≤1e−8`. Record the signed shell and beam side works even when they cancel. The matrix products `W_shell=−λᵀCs qs` and `W_beam=λᵀCb qb` sum to `λᵀCq` **identically** and are not a third independent gate. For implementation parity, separately accumulate both works over reference overlay quadrature without using assembled `C` and require `|(W_shell,quad+W_beam,quad)−λᵀCq|/E0≤1e−8`. An accurately solved weak equation by itself does not bound work for arbitrarily large multipliers. For beam load quadrature, retain analytic total resultant/moment checks and additionally require each *assembled* 16-point versus 24-point load entry to agree after scaling translation rows by `F0` and Hermite slope/rotation rows by `M0`, with maximum difference `≤1e−10`. These are proposed pre-registration checks, not evidence already executed.

The stock CalculiX 2.23 B31/CPS four-sample collocation cases remain **auxiliary analogues**: the known 12-case mesh study did not settle physical convergence, B31 does not match the stated generalized beam shear section, and nodal `*EQUATION` ties are not the P0/P1 variational integral. A closer U1 `GENERAL` planar shear section mixed with CPS fails in the stock executable. A future matched planar external route would require a source-pinned patched U1/CPS binary with a mixed-element canary and independently integrated weak equations, row-rank/pivot checks, both-axis refinement, signed load integration, grouped reaction/energy/work recovery and a separately frozen deck/binary identity. This planar route cannot discharge the required out-of-plane torque/footprint, curved or nonlinear external cases, nor the full eccentric six-channel comparison. Executable availability and the G0A plate smoke are not numerical agreement.
