## Resolve the multiplier trend without weakening the screen

At fixed `(Ns,Ny,Nb)=(128,64,512)`, the natural Q4/CST-CB P1
Nm16→32→64 series changed Q4 left-support Fy by `+35,958.083 N`, then
`+15,520.863 N`. The terminal fixed-scale gap is also still responsive.
No multiplier resolution policy follows from these three levels.

Before any new solve, derive and review the expected interface resolution
ratio and check local/global constraint rank, matrix pattern growth and
the physical meaning of Nm128 for the same bonded line. Extend only the
diagnostic runner and independent line overlay admission from Nm64 to
Nm128 if that review passes. Keep the native product formulation and
existing qualification records untouched. Add manufactured P1 integration,
union-knot, finite-work and rank preflights at Nm128, including the
existing `Nb=512` and both shell hosts. P1 has `3(Nm+1)=387` multiplier
rows at Nm128, compared with 195 at Nm64; with the present 36,223 free
physical coordinates, the expected KKT dimension is 36,610. Beam/mortar
element ratio is 4:1 and longitudinal shell/mortar ratio is 1:1. Verify
those actual counts, all four offset hosts and exact-rational overlay.
Freeze a dimensionless row/column scale, rank threshold, norm-scaled
interface stability measure and sparse/bounded-local rank procedure
before execution. A raw mixed-unit singular value or a global dense SVD
is not sufficient, and full row rank alone does not prove stable coupling.
A setup or rank failure is
evidence to preserve, not reason to solve with a penalty or silently
change the mesh ratio.

Register two new, single-use natural cases `(128,64,512,128)`, Q4 and
CST-CB, with unchanged geometry, offsets, smooth load, P1 law, supports,
scales and sparse backend. Freeze exact source and dependencies, one-thread
identity, output roots, initial expected counts, 8 GiB/900 s per case
or a separately reviewed budget, supervisor failure policy and a
read-only adjudicator **before** execution. Run a source-matched Nm64
control only if the **pre-execution registration** explicitly proves reuse
of the present source, state schema, dependencies, runner and raw hashes;
otherwise freeze fresh, single-use Q4/CST-CB Nm64 controls first. In
either route require stringent 23-channel and G2/Gmotion parity against
the present roots before the Nm128 cases. Do not decide control reuse
after seeing Nm128 results. Never relabel or overwrite an older root.

Adjudicate physical equilibrium, weak and continuous gap, support and
port wrenches, work/energy, KKT size/fill and root elastic/direct/external
terms from retained `q,λ`, without re-solving. Compare Nm32→64 and
64→128 signed changes for both topologies, at all fixed physical stations
and first-quarter bands. Freeze a complete channel-specific normalized
comparison and absolute floors before execution, including all 23
historical response channels, G2/Gmotion, support bands, root terms and
port integrals. As a proposed diagnostic screen, call the fine step
material if any registered signed response exceeds the existing draft
1% Cauchy measure with its fixed floors, if terminal G2 exceeds 0.01,
or if the registered G2 contraction criterion fails; independently
review that rule before registration. Keep free equilibrium ≤`1e-8`,
weak rows ≤`1e-9`, global balance ≤`1e-8` and work/energy ≤`1e-8 E0`
as mandatory numerical checks. A still-material fine step keeps the
multiplier resolution question open; a small step is only a finite
screen, not uniform inf-sup or continuum convergence. Do not infer
asymptotic order from one new level.

## Independent physical target and other axes

The old 1% Q4/CST-CB criterion is a draft matched-discretization screen,
not an executed external solver comparison. The CalculiX B31/CPS route
uses a different beam shear/attachment idealization, and the attempted
mixed U1/CPS route failed in the stock executable. Before a replacement
physical comparison, match shell and beam sections, offsets, coupling
work, load measures and support reactions, demonstrate executable mixed
elements, then freeze deck/binary identities, executed command/log and
parsed observables, normalization and thresholds, and at least three
independently mesh-checked levels with a stable physical trend. Retain
the failed stock mixed U1/CPS evidence. A resolved-shell model within ANYsolver
remains an internal comparator. Do not describe a generated deck or
successful process exit as numerical agreement.

The Ns64→128 and Ny32→64 natural response changes remain material.
After the multiplier policy is understood, preregister an upper
longitudinal/transverse study with feasible resource limits and at least
three systematic levels plus stable trend/order evidence for a convergence
claim. Keep changing shell
stiffness/support partition and changing trace/port distinguishable;
reuse the prior fixed-mesh 2×2 operator result as a diagnostic control.

No production line law, multiplier space, G0B acceptance or G1 entry is
authorized by this design. A next registration must receive independent
mechanics and evidence-chain review before its first case.
