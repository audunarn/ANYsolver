# Conditional E4-PL linear qualification plan

## Authority

This is the only immediately executable successor plan authorized by E4-0 terminal
`PROVISIONAL_GO_E4_PL_LINEAR_QUALIFICATION_PLAN`. It is a plan, not an
executed qualification and not a candidate registration. Its future execution
must start from the accepted E4-0 closeout commit and must freeze a newly named
candidate identity before any outcome is observed.

A broader jointly coupled energetic/dual weak-symmetry architecture remains
an unclassified new identity. E4-0 neither disproves nor authorizes that route;
it would require its own separately named governing plan.

The frozen predecessor is
`study_e4_pl.wg2020_surface_reduced_perturbed_lagrange_v1`: the flat-affine
WG2020 `n=7`, `k=0`, MITC-shear core with the dimensionally reduced
three-mode perturbed-Lagrange drill functional and the independently separated
affine residual-mode energy. No coefficient, interpolation, quadrature, or
recovery convention may be changed while retaining that predecessor identity.

## Qualification scope

The next bounded program may qualify linear static behavior only. It must:

1. freeze a quantitative admissible non-affine Q4 geometry class, including
   positive-Jacobian, shape-regularity, aspect, skew, and warpage limits;
2. derive the geometry-dependent source hourglass vector and all covariant
   transformations without post-outcome selection;
3. prove local mixed-block invertibility and stationary mixed/condensed parity
   over the admitted geometry and isotropic-material domain;
4. establish rank 18 and exactly six rigid modes for every admitted element;
5. preregister an assembled norm, support class, mesh families, and a genuine
   mesh-independent stability/coercivity proof obligation;
6. execute exact patch tests plus distortion and thickness sequences through
   at least `h/L=10^-6`, with locking and category-drift terminals frozen
   before results;
7. exercise the existing S235/S275/S355/S420/S460 records using only
   `StructuralMaterial + thickness`, with zero new drill/material inputs;
   preserve RP-C208 material provenance separately from July-2025 RU-SHIP
   project-edition metadata and describe the result only as “compatible with
   DNV analysis workflows,” never DNV-approved; and
8. keep PL multiplier, deleted-mode, and hourglass quantities outside physical
   `N/M/Q`, stress, yield, fatigue, and code-check recovery; and
9. preregister outcome-independent limits and mesh-refinement trends for the
   ratios of PL/hourglass energy to physical energy and for drill-mode
   participation. Exceeding a limit rejects or narrows the candidate scope;
   it may never tune `gamma_PL=G` or `epsilon_hg=10^-3`.

The source `2 x 2` rule remains normative. A preregistered higher-order rule
may be used only as a sensitivity classifier on non-affine cases; it cannot
replace the candidate rule or tune coefficients.

Physical loads remain in `range(T5)`. Direct nodal drilling/normal moments are
ineligible in this wave. Work-equivalent drill moments, shell intersections,
beam coupling, and any moment-transfer claim require a separately named
extension.

## Required early screens

Execution must stop on the first certified failure of:

- source and formulation identity;
- positive Jacobian and covariant transformation closure;
- local internal-block invertibility;
- exact rigid covariance and rank/nullspace;
- mixed/condensed energy, work, residual, tangent, load, and recovery parity;
- mesh-family stability/coercivity;
- membrane, bending, transverse-shear, or drilling locking; or
- separation of numerical drill diagnostics from physical resultants.

Diagnostic coefficient sweeps around `gamma_PL=G` and
`epsilon_hg=10^-3` remain hostile controls only. They may reveal sensitivity
but may not select a value or rescue the frozen identity.

## Deferred programs

Generalized sections, orthotropy, laminates, geometric stiffness, eigenvalue
buckling, finite rotations, nonlinear response, mass, modal/transient
dynamics, coupling, legacy parity, performance, and production integration
remain separately named dependent programs. A linear GO cannot authorize any
of them. Density may be recorded as existing material metadata but is unused
in this linear-static wave, and no independent drilling inertia is admitted.

No public API, selector, serialization, export, dispatch, default, package,
workflow, or production source change is part of this plan. Every outcome
retains `NO_GO_PRODUCTION_RESTRICTION_UNCHANGED`; legacy `ShellElement` remains
the production default until a later implementation/integration qualification
is independently accepted.
