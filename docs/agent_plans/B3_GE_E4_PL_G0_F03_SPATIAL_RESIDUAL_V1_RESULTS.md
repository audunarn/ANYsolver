## What was measured

The original n12/n16/n24 q/eta states and accepted exact pose polynomials
were used without changing a state. Independent tensor Legendre fields
provide shell stress/divergence. A separately constructed finite-square
adjoint transfers the line density and both endpoint atoms into area loads.
Direct monomial pad moments provide an independent trace for 27 virtual
fields. Three work routes, physical reactions, local/global wrenches and
integration by parts were compared over 45 fixed cells and 28 boundary
segments, using both registered Gauss orders 48 and 64.

The following are sums of the registered dimensionless **squared residual
measures**. They are not relative solution errors or energy-error bounds.

| n24 | 11.5379498875 | 0.00454445134237 | 1.16516113281e-13 |

Both totals decrease for these saved states. This observation alone does
not establish physical convergence; strong L2 residuals do not supply a
solution-error estimate here. The earlier energy, reaction and functional
settling requirements remain the acceptance criteria.

The most persistent volume concentrations include cells 12 and 30:
x approximately 0.15–0.25 m, y approximately −0.28–−0.18 m and
0.18–0.28 m. These coincide with the two positive-x root attachment pads.
Other material contributions remain in adjacent cells; the map does not
isolate one cause of the remaining physical discrepancy.

At n24, the two rightmost free-edge segments, x approximately 1.25–1.5 m,
contain 85.4% of the free-edge squared defect. They meet the right support
corners. The upper and lower maps agree to numerical precision for this
symmetric reference geometry. This is evidence of where approximation
work should be investigated, not permission to drop other cells or checks.

Reconstructed reactions agree with the original saved reactions to about
`1.3e-9` in the corresponding force/moment units. Exact functional-density
coefficients differ from the old reconstructed coefficients by at most
`1.95e-8` in their corresponding channel units; atom differences are below
`1.17e-10`. These small descriptive changes do not waive the separate saved
endpoint-action failures or qualify the reference.
