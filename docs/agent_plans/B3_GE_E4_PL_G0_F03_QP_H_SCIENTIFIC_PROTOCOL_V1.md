## Adjudication

First verify source, registration, file inventory, case order, hashes, schemas, dimensions, finite data, exact grid bits and terminal receipts. Missing, duplicate, stale or extra evidence is `FAILED_EVIDENCE`. Then independently bind every signed case value to its saved q/eta, including terminal atom ownership, Decimal endpoint resultants, all eight physical band wrenches at 48/64 order, the saved cosine coefficient and a conservative bound for every one-ulp interval. The parent reconstructs the complete functional and the two **coefficient-space difference** norms; comparing operand norms alone is insufficient.

For each of the 49 signed components, require both successive pairwise relative changes `|fine−coarse|/max(|fine|,|coarse|,1e-3*S)` at most **1%**, with frozen force/moment/pose/energy scale `S`, and final absolute change at most the preceding change plus `1e-5*S`. For both complete H1-dual functional differences, require `distance/max(norm_coarse,norm_fine,1000 N)` at most **1%**, with the second distance no greater than the first plus **10 N**. The 48/64 signed density and cross-term resolution is `1e-10`. Preserve all component values and failures. Verify X0→X1 and X1→X2 prolongation, nonnegative difference energy, monotone Ritz energy within `1e-10*E0`, and load/root/tip/energy identities within `1e-10`. Independently bind actual and nominal load resultants/moments, separate shell and beam integration, raw physical and represented-adjoint origin moments, endpoint atoms, and total versus line-only wrenches. The area/line nodal and area-order limits remain `2e-10`; free equilibrium, area/line wrench, support lifting, global force/moment balance, root reaction and signed spatial work remain **`2e-9`**, with wrench scales `(F0,F0,F0*0.46)` and virtual-work scale `E0`. No small energy or scalar subset substitutes for the complete holdout.

Terminal precedence is: `FAILED_EVIDENCE` for identity/inventory/semantic invalidity; `FAILED_EXECUTION` for launch, resource, cancellation or incomplete case; `FAILED_NUMERICAL_SCREEN` for a completed violated numerical predicate; `UNRESOLVED_NUMERICAL_SCREEN` for a complete but unavailable/indeterminate bound; `UNRESOLVED_PHYSICAL_SETTLING` for a completed numerically valid run that fails the physical h criteria; and `PASSED_BOUNDED_CLASSICAL_REFERENCE` only when every required predicate passes. Independent postexecution replay and mechanics review must accept the exact installed artifacts and raw evidence before any pass is promoted. A pass is evidence for the classical reference alone.
