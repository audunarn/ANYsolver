# F03 classical CST checkerboard experiment — prospective design

22 September 2026. **Pre-execution formulation design; no candidate result or qualification.** The frozen fixed-diagonal CST loaded screen at `55033e7` remains immutable, including its nine adverse Q4/CST draft comparison channels in `B3_GE_E4_PL_G0_F03_CLASSICAL_LOADED_RESOURCE_SCREEN.md`. The experiment tests one mechanically motivated hypothesis: a uniformly BL→TR triangulated plate breaks the panel's y-reflection symmetry and may contribute spurious axial response to the vertically loaded line. It does not assume the beam-energy or all 1% discrepancies will disappear.

Keep the old `CST` topology and evidence unchanged. Add a separate private `CST-CB` identity: for cell `(i,j)`, use BL→TR CCW triangles `(bl,br,tr),(bl,tr,tl)` when `(i+j)` is even, and BR→TL CCW triangles `(bl,br,tl),(br,tr,tl)` otherwise. All proposed reference inventory `Ny` values are even. Across y reflection `j→Ny−1−j`, parity reverses and the diagonal reflects; across x reflection `i→2Ns−1−i`, parity also reverses. This is a property of the panel mesh, not a symmetry of the complete root-clamped beam problem. An odd-`Ny` checkerboard does not claim exact y-reflection; use even `Ny` for this experiment, and keep historical odd-`Ny` old-CST smoke distinct.

Update the triangle host selection and exact/floating reference overlays together. For a falling BR→TL diagonal the split is `ξ+η=1` in cell coordinates and the fixed-y crossing is `ξ=1−η`; for a rising BL→TR diagonal it remains `η=ξ`. The exact-rational host uses stored node axes and rational inequalities, with independent branch checks around every crossing. The generic barycentric pose and local CST stiffness still use CCW vertex order. Do not alter Q4, beam, material, load, mortar test, scale, physical thresholds or the old `CST` identity.
