## Work and supported target space

\[

\]

Then F_lambda(P w)=F_lambda(w) for every density: force means are preserved by transferring v-U into the shell; shell director/arm means are preserved; all beam integrated cell moments are unchanged. ## Norm bound

Write h_U=L^{-1}int(|U|²+ell²|U'|²) and h_Theta=ell² L^{-1}int|Theta|². For the area-normalized rectangular shell, y-uniform E U gives exactly
\[
 h_S(EU,0)=h_U(U).
\]
The shell scalar norm is the same on each translation; each director component has an extra common factor ell², so the identical Pi_S has the same C_S on all five fields. Hence
\[
 h_S(\Pi_S(v-EU),\Pi_S\eta)
 \le 2C_S^2h_S(v,\eta)+2C_S^2h_U(U).
\]
For rho=max_surface |a|/ell and K=max(2,1+2rho²), including the declared finite-width averaging,
\[
 h_B(0,\Theta)\le(1+\rho^2)h_\Theta,\qquad
 h_U+h_\Theta\le K h_B(U,\Theta).
\]
Combining these gives the proposed bound
\[
 h({\cal P}w)\le
 2C_S^2h_S+
 K\max(2C_S^2,1+\rho^2)h_B
 \le C_{\rm product}^2h(w),
\]
\[
 C_{\rm product}^2=
 \max\{2C_S^2,\ K\max(2C_S^2,1+\rho^2)\}.
\]
Arm covariance is included in the averaged square and does not weaken these bounds. Positive beam Theta_z remains included even when the centerline BL5 law annihilates it.

For the independently declared exact dual M=M_S+M_B and the restricted native positive primal norm, moment preservation and this norm bound give
