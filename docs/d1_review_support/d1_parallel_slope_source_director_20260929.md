# Parallel inclined shell press — bounded source-director discriminator

This development-only experiment tested whether inclining both the four-Q4 source and two-S3 deformable target by a common 0.1 reference slope would produce resolved normal contact work on free source `ry` coordinates. The physical top/bottom skins begin with a 40 µm gap along the common normal. Material, 50 µm penetration budget, fine Q1 pressure, monolithic enforcement, left-edge source `ry` clamp and all other constraints remain those of the [prior free-source test](d1_coupled_source_director_20260929.md).

**Preflight.** The contact-free constrained stiffness has positive scaled minimum 3.3331e−4. At 0.0005 N, its predicted minimum sampled gap is −15.302 µm, within the declared budget, and maximum source `ry` is 75.45 µrad. The actual initial physical gap and conservative lower bound both resolve to 40 µm. [Raw preflight](d1_eccentric_source_preflight_20260929.json) and [bounded resource](d1_eccentric_source_preflight_resource_20260929.json): 1.75 s/157.79 MB.

**Coupled result.** The ordinary static route accepted one 0.0005 N increment in [raw result](d1_eccentric_source_first_event_20260929.json), under [7.54 s/274.39 MB resource use](d1_eccentric_source_first_event_resource_20260929.json). Two pressure modes are active; maximum source `ry` is 69.94 µrad and maximum unloaded free-`ry` residual is 2.16e−18 N·m. The source contact `ry` moment is only 7.61e−13 N·m, versus 7.01e−13 N·m in the flat case. The solver-reported sampled minimum gap is −5.33 µm. This is not a resolved source-director coupling or an independent geometric gap check.

**Interpretation.** The common inclination left the two physical normals parallel. A rotation of the source director produces skin motion proportional to `e_y × n_source`, whose dot product with the parallel contact normal is identically zero. The near-null contact moment is therefore expected for frictionless parallel planar contact. An inclination of both bodies cannot discriminate nonzero normal source-director work. A future nonzero-moment fixture needs different interacting normals or curvature, while retaining valid initial geometry, an admissible path and an independent constrained response. No larger force sweep follows from this result.
