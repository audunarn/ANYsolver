## Three consequential equations and claim limits

1. **Return projection is a trial diagnostic, not dissipated energy.** In the present law (`s3_sliding_friction.py:483-531`), `return_storage_drop = (k/2)(|e_trial|²-|e_return|²)` and `D=μ p_end |Δanchor_tangent|`; `return_projection_loss=max(0, return_storage_drop-D)`. Consider a scalar constant-normal/pressure slider with `k=1000 Pa/m`, cap `μp=1 Pa`, old elastic extension 0 and imposed material displacement 0.003 m. The return gives `e_return=.001 m`, plastic slip `.002 m`, `D=.002 Pa·m`, and stored-energy rise `.0005 Pa·m`. Exact quasistatic traction work is `.0025 Pa·m`; hypothetical `return_storage_drop=.004 Pa·m` and `projection_loss=.002 Pa·m`. Adding either diagnostic to accepted-state dissipation would invent or double-count energy. A separate independent scalar traction-path integration should be the local oracle, not a rearrangement of the production energy fields.

2. **The finite-gap power split is jointly objective, not termwise invariant.** Existing complete work is `De=P(Js-Jt)−[n⊗r+gI]Dn`, `g=n·r` (`s3_sliding_friction.py:393-409`), and the history report splits the endpoint contraction into `t·P dr` and `−g t·Dn` (`s3_friction_history_path.py:41-65`). For `n=z`, `r=e*x+g*z`, `t=−k e*x`, and common infinitesimal rotation about y, `P dr=gω*x+…`, `dn=ω*x`: the material-labelled part is `−k e g ω` and the numerical normal part `+k e g ω`; only the sum vanishes. Retain both terms and test common rigid motion. Calling the first term alone objective physical slip work would be incorrect at finite gap.

3. **Endpoint pressure times returned slip is a discrete rule.** Current `D=μp_end |Δanchor_tangent|` is not generally `∫μp(s)dγ`; if pressure rises linearly from 0 to `p1` during a fixed plastic slip increment, the latter is half the endpoint product, and a pressure-zero release can omit earlier within-step slip. A constant-pressure slider validates the rule only in its controlled limit. Actual coupled 2/4 subincrement comparison can measure resolution sensitivity, but does not turn endpoint products or stage traction trapezoids into exact integrals. Keep release of old storage separate from Coulomb dissipation and from the hypothetical return projection loss.

The old finite-resolution pressure event fixture established release/rebirth but had virtually zero material endpoint contraction (~1e-25 J); the new fixture must demonstrate nontrivial tangent work through actual accepted coupled states.
