# Accepted tilted-pose source rotational work — development replay

An existing eight-Q4, fixed-S3-target press at target reference slope `dz/dx=1e-5` had completed three increments with six active pressure modes. Its 83.9 kB [saved result](d1_tilted_saved_press_20260928.json) was copied from the retained development report so the replay and oracle do not require another 126-second nonlinear solve. The source bottom skin has a 25 mm offset, and source `ry` is prescribed by the guide. The previously reported source-x tangent and case limitations remain in the [original record](../reference_cases/general_contact_d1_tilted_supported_press_development.md).

At the saved final pose, the current contact kernel exactly replays all 18 pressure coefficients. Its [source moment screen](d1_saved_tilted_source_moment_b_20260929.json) resolves nonzero generalized source `ry` contact moments: largest nodal magnitude `5.06485e-9 N·m`, sum `-2.67292807532314e-8 N·m`. The current structural reassembly gives summed source `ry` internal force `-0.05692247030108157 N·m`; adding contact gives `-0.056922497030362326 N·m`, identical to the saved source-guide `ry` reaction. This is a constrained reaction, not an unprescribed rotational equilibrium equation. The structural contribution is about 2.13 million times the contact contribution, so the total-reaction match alone is a weak discriminator for contact work. The bounded screen used 5.56 s and 264.96 MB [resource](d1_saved_tilted_source_moment_resource_b_20260929.json). The earlier source-only screen and its [resource](d1_saved_tilted_source_moment_resource_20260929.json) are retained; the second screen adds structural reaction reconstruction.

The [independent planar-work oracle](d1_tilted_source_work_oracle.py) does not call a contact or shell work kernel. It integrates first-order source pressure support analytically over the reference overlap: `x` is eight equal Q4 cells over `[0.1,1.1] m`; the target covers all source `x`; source `y=[0.1,1.1] m` is clipped at target `y=1.0 m`. The lower and upper row integrals are `0.495` and `0.405 m`; x edge/interior integrals are `0.0625/0.125 m`. It also assembles the exact tensor-product Q1 pressure mass: the x-cell matrix is `h[[1/3,1/6],[1/6,1/3]]`, and the clipped y matrix is `[[0.333,0.162],[0.162,0.243]]`. From the saved numerical pressure coefficients, it obtains the normal resultant and independently maps both a uniform source `ry` variation and each source director row through the physical bottom skin against the target normal `(-s,0,1)/√(1+s²)`.

The oracle takes the saved pressure coefficients as input; it independently checks geometric support integration, normal resultant and each source skin contact-work row at this pose. Read-only mechanics review confirmed the exact Q1 integrals, node ordering and contact-residual sign. The reaction reconstruction combines this prediction with production structural internal work, so it is mainly an assembly consistency check and does not independently verify the structural reaction. It does not independently solve the coupled structural/contact equilibrium, verify free source-`ry` response or verify source tangent rows. The S3 target is fixed.
