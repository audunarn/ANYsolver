# D1 fine-Q1 pressure repair: exact-law boundary

**29 September 2026. Read-only independent mechanics assessment; no production law changed.** This extends the [two-hat Q4/S3 counterexample](d1_cut_front_two_hat_counterexample_20260929.md) and the [bounded repair screen](d1_pressure_repair_design_20260929.md). It identifies what a Q4-compatible pressure repair must change. It does not qualify a repair or every admitted contact geometry.

## Question and result

Can a load-independent multiplier block cure the cut-front pressure-work loss while retaining (1) the present bilinear Q4/S3 physical work map, (2) every positive-support fine-Q1 pressure hat as an independent nonnegative unknown, and (3) exact geometric-gap complementarity on the active set? **No, under those three assumptions.** This is a finite-dimensional statement; it does not exclude a separately derived stabilization that changes the discrete law but converges to the same continuum contact problem.

Let pressure coefficients `lambda_i` have units Pa, and define integrated gaps `b_i(u)=integral_Gamma(u) N_i g(u) dA` [m³]. At a touching zero-gap pose, `B_h=db/du` [m²], so `B_h^T lambda` is a nodal force [N]. The exhibited signed, interior-feasible pressure direction `c` has discrete structural energy-dual work `||B_h^T c||_(K_h^-1)=O(theta³)`. An admissible, fixed-energy continuum shell motion has work `Omega(theta²)`. Squared response energies therefore scale as `O(theta⁶)` and at least `Omega(theta⁴)`, respectively. The saved construction supplies the geometry, exact integrals, production work-map checks, and positive 1-Pa baseline whose `+/-0.5-Pa*c` perturbations stay in the pressure cone.

Suppose a load-independent linear pressure block `S lambda` is added to the *active* integrated-gap equation. At the touching pose, any strictly positive pressure vector in a neighborhood can be balanced by a manufactured nodal load `f=B_h^T lambda`. Preserving the unchanged active equation `b=0` for this open positive cone requires `S lambda=0` throughout it, hence `S=0`. A pure change of pressure coordinates, force scaling, or Newton solver also leaves `B_h` and the counterexample intact. A nonlinear residual that vanishes at the exact continuum solution may be asymptotically consistent, but it still changes the finite-dimensional active law and needs a new consistency/stability argument. Positivity alone does not remove this mode.

## A physical subscale candidate, with a different identity

One derivable route is a local space `W` of independent physical relative shell displacement responses, orthogonalized against the existing discrete shell space in the actual structural energy. Let `K_W` [N/m] be its positive energy matrix and `D_ia=integral_Gamma N_i n dot phi_a dA` [m²] its exact overlap work map. Eliminating its displacement coordinates gives `S_phys=D K_W^-1 D^T` [m³/Pa] in pressure coordinates; `lambda^T S_phys lambda` has units J. For nodal forces `q_i=M_i lambda_i` [N], where `M_i=integral_Gamma N_i dA` [m²], the corresponding map is `D_q=M^-1 D` and `S_q=D_q K_W^-1 D_q^T` [m/N]. The exhibited mode could gain `Omega(theta⁴)` response energy if `W` contains a uniformly bounded-energy witness for it.

That Schur term represents **physical subscale compliance**. The active gap includes the subscale motion, so the contact surface's effective displacement is no longer the unchanged qualified bilinear Q4 skin even if the reference overlap geometry is Q4. It requires a distinct structural/contact formulation identity. The corrected 37-field E4-PL bubble preflight is one local example of a possible physical subscale, not an implemented moving-contact method. Constant traction is *not* in the null of a physical compliance term; its response must be checked against an independent continuum reference rather than assumed unchanged. Pressure positivity remains coefficientwise, but complementarity applies to the augmented physical gap.

Moving overlap requires consistent derivatives of `Gamma`, support, `D` and the subscale energy. The overlap-boundary terms vanish from the first variation at the flat zero-gap pose used in the counterexample; they generally survive at nonzero gap and in the tangent. Tiny positive support must be retained; a force-coordinate implementation must avoid dividing by vanishing `M_i`. Detecting this one mode does not prove cut-uniform stability across all pressure directions. That would need a uniform lower spectral bound against an independently resolved physical response over the declared cut family, plus the existing gap, response, tangent, integration and restart gates.

The rejected full-reference gradient jump remains `O(1)` on the exhibited mode while its physical squared work is `O(theta⁴)`. A specially cut-scaled or residual-consistent stabilization could be explored, but it would change discrete complementarity and needs its own derivation, traction patch checks and cut-uniform evidence. No stabilization or subscale is admitted by this assessment.

The method families have primary research precedents in [Burman, Hansbo and Larson's augmented-Lagrangian contact analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.03326) and [Moretto, Franceschini and Ferronato's mortar traction-jump stabilization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18905). Their geometries, spaces and proofs do not qualify this moving shell mortar.

**Next decision:** preserve the current Q4 geometry by deriving and qualifying a stabilization with changed discrete complementarity, or adopt a separately qualified physical subscale/enriched shell profile. Neither is a solver-only patch to the existing D1 law. The user-facing choice remains pending; do not replay expensive coupled cases until a complete candidate exists.
