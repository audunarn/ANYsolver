## Actionable finding: swept admission omits reciprocal cap compatibility

P2, beam_chain_ends.py:168-176. _endpoint_work rejects an active source cap whose closest physical target cap does not choose it back (lines 81-85). _swept_admissible calls admissible_chain_endpoint_path separately for each endpoint; this helper checks fixed nearest features, allowed material nodes and strict source ownership, but explicitly leaves cap reciprocity to its caller. The caller supplies no reciprocal pair check. Thus its True result can admit an increment whose final endpoint evaluation raises ValueError rather than receiving the normal bounded cutback.

Concrete source-derived counterexample (not executed): all coordinates are planar with z=0, combined radius 1.001, gap tolerance .01. Selected A chain is S=(0,.002) -> Q=(-2,0) -> P=(0,0). Selected B chain initially is R=(.1,1.01) -> T=(.1,3.01) -> U=(.1,4.01). Translate every B node down .015. Both chains have two genuine material ends; the initial pose is open and every segment remains regular.

At the final pose R=(.1,.995), P chooses R: distance squared 1.000025, strict target clamp and source ownership -.2. R instead chooses S: distance squared .996049, strict target clamp and source ownership -1.986. S chooses R and has negative ownership too. The P/R choice is therefore nonreciprocal and _endpoint_work rejects it. The nearest targets, projections and ownership signs remain strict and fixed through the translation. All-target opening handles the initially open intervals, and fixed-branch certificates can handle the closed remainder. Minimum final gap is approximately -.00298, within .01. The whole-gap speed bound .015 resolves after subdivision; pairwise distributed plateau checks are also strict. Nothing in _swept_admissible excludes this nonreciprocal activation.

A matching path-level reciprocal admission check must retain a bounded, fail-closed treatment of activation and reciprocal partners throughout the path. At minimum add a regression connecting an initially open manager, swept_increment_admissible/admissible_step_fraction, and final evaluation for this counterexample. The existing direct _endpoint_work nonreciprocal test proves rejection at a pose, but cannot prove swept admission consistency. No incorrect contact force acceptance was established: final evaluation does reject, and nonlinear_static also evaluates before converged acceptance. The identified consequence is an inconsistent admission promise and possible escaping trial evaluation failure instead of controlled cutback.
