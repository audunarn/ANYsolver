## Successor execution contract

The successor runner exposes an explicit correction-inheritance mode.  It is
valid only for `lane=rehearsal` with either `partition-id=R-GUARDS` or inert union
finalization.  All other combinations fail before mechanics imports or output
creation.

Before a successor guard child is launched, the runner shall:

1. verify the clean successor commit, exact allowed extent, correction addendum,
   independent design review, and accepted successor implementation review;
2. verify the failed process record and exact node-157 failure described above;
3. derive the predecessor expected candidate, full input DAG, and review bytes
   from the hash-bound accepted smoke evidence;
4. require that derived authority exactly equals the frozen predecessor
   commit/tree/review and recursively validate smoke, local, R-HISTORY, and
   R-PREFIX-A through D under it;
5. require that the successor differs from the predecessor only in the reviewed
   correction, correction authority, runner, runner tests, and completion-register
   paths; and
6. use only the validated predecessor R-HISTORY packet outputs as immutable
   inputs to the successor guard probes.

The successor `R-GUARDS` aggregate contains only nodes 90 through 233 under the
successor candidate.  Its receipt binds the seven inherited evidence descriptors,
the predecessor authority, the correction authority, and the failed process
incident.  It never rewrites or relabels inherited bytes.

The inert correction union binds the seven inherited descriptors and the accepted
successor guard descriptor.  Its ordered record list contains the unmodified
predecessor records 0 through 89 followed by the unmodified successor records 90
through 233.  Each record retains its own candidate identity.  The union records
both candidate identities and verifies exact 234-record coverage and order.

## Negative guards and terminals

Tests must reject wrong predecessor candidate/review/input hashes, altered
accepted files, reordered or omitted prerequisites, a different failed incident,
extra files or reparse entries, correction mode on another partition/lane,
mixed predecessor chains, changed record bytes, and any attempt to hide the two
candidate identities.

The only successful terminals are:

- `COMPLETE_GE_BEAM3_G3C_PHYSICAL_CORRECTED_GUARDS_PARTITION_ONLY`; and
- `COMPLETE_GE_BEAM3_G3C_PHYSICAL_CORRECTED_REHEARSAL_ONLY`.

Both retain `full_g3c_qualified=false` and `production_qualified=false`.
Failure yields process evidence only and blocks the correction union.  This
addendum authorizes no formal run, MO08/MO18 closure, production qualification,
