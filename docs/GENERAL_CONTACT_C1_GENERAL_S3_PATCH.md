# General nodal S3 patch mortar development route

`S3_PATCH_GENERAL_NODAL_PRIMAL_MORTAR_PENALTY_STATIC_DEV_V0` admits an explicit pair of physical S3 V2D shell patches, with one or two triangular faces per side and at least three faces overall. The pressure side is named by the interaction. It works through ordinary von Karman nonlinear static force/displacement control and the multi-control contact path owner. The path has its own `S3_PATCH_GENERAL_NODAL_MULTI_CONTROL_PENALTY_PATH_DEV_V0` identity. These are opt-in development profiles, not C1 engineering acceptance.

Each selected face has a declared physical side and formulation-owned thickness offset. The source and target use disjoint material nodes. Adjacent faces on one side may share nodes; the physical skin positions and work maps at those nodes must agree. The solver retains one physical coordinate triplet per distinct node and one nonnegative P1 numerical pressure coefficient per distinct **source** node. A maximum of four face pairs is admitted, with a finite differentiation budget. Larger patches fail resource admission explicitly; no candidates are dropped.

For each projected source–target overlap, the kernel integrates P1 support, weighted normal gap and pressure Gram blocks over the source **reference-area** measure. It sums those quantities at shared material nodes **before** computing normalized gaps and the penalty energy. Each source face contributes to one pressure coefficient at its shared corner; internal triangulation seams do not create duplicate pressure unknowns. The tangent differentiates the same aggregate energy with respect to unique physical node coordinates. Internal polygon combinatorics can switch as nonmatching triangulation lines cross; a targeted step-refinement check found stable aggregate work through one such switch. True within-surface projected overlap is rejected.

The manager separately reports numerical pressure/weighted gaps and conservative physical lower gap. The latter checks every vertex pair of every declared source–target face pair along the current source face normal. It can reject safe poses when those projected faces do not actually overlap, but cannot hide a crossing. A swept Bézier bound covers the linearized path of all physical corners; unresolved normals or gaps cause cutback or failure. This development bound has not been qualified for folded patches, large director rotation or general self-contact.

The runnable [general_contact_s3_patch_path.py](../examples/general_contact_s3_patch_path.py) presses, slides 50 mm, opens and recontacts two nonmatching two-face S3 patches through one static path. The final route has four source pressure nodes and a version 2 checkpoint. Its 20 mm gap budget and soft 1000 Pa/m penalty are deliberately permissive to exercise the integrated route; neither is a recommended engineering setting. In the measured example, only one pressure DOF is active during pressing/recontact, and the conservative physical lower gap reaches about −6.9 mm. The open phase returns zero contact force. A midpoint checkpoint resumes to the same final displacement within the focused test tolerance.

The local checks cover exact shared-node reference weights, positive Gram rank for a full two-triangle source patch, nonuniform skin work and global force balance. They also retain the earlier single-face weak-gap counterexample: positive normalized P1 gaps can coexist with negative physical gap. Pressure-space convergence, tight independent gap enforcement, arbitrary partial contact, large patch search, friction and impact remain required before the plan's first usable handoff. The complete C0–C9 programme remains open.

## Projected normal development profile

`S3_PATCH_GENERAL_NODAL_PRIMAL_MORTAR_AUGMENTED_STATIC_DEV_V0` is an opt-in S3-source/S3-target patch profile owned by the multi-control static path. Declare `normal_law="augmented"`, dimensional `normal_stiffness` in Pa/m, dimensional `gap_tolerance` in m, and `pressure_tolerance` in Pa. The pressure side is fixed by the explicit interaction. Q4 and direct force/displacement owners remain on their existing penalty profiles.

At a fixed multiplier estimate, each supported source pressure node uses `p_i = max(0, lambda_i - epsilon W_i/S_i)`, with `W_i` the reference weighted gap and `S_i` its positive reference support. Contact residual is `-sum_i p_i dW_i/dq`. Its tangent includes derivatives of the moving overlap and support; it may be nonsymmetric. The nested path updates `lambda_i` after equilibrium, resets it when support disappears, and commits only after the declared pressure and physical-gap checks pass. Multipliers are algorithmic state stored in the version-2 path checkpoint.

The reported `augmented_merit_j` is a numerical merit, not contact storage. `penalty_energy` is zero for this profile. At zero gap, a held constant pressure gives normal work and no frictionless lateral work when the overlap area changes. The [augmented patch example](../examples/general_contact_s3_patch_augmented_path.py) exercises a multiplier update and sliding; its soft regularization and 20 mm gap tolerance are development settings. The measured gap and pressure-space stability still need a registered engineering qualification before C1 acceptance.

A retained two-by-two S3 patch counterexample makes the pressure-resolution limit explicit (`s3-augmented-weak-open1.txt`). At a trial pose, all four normalized source gaps are positive, so every projected pressure and multiplier update is zero, while the independent conservative physical lower gap is about −0.32 mm. The declared 50 µm gap gate fails. Raising the augmentation count cannot repair a pressure space that does not see this penetration; the model needs adequate contact resolution or a separately derived enforcement method. The existing path refuses to accept such a pose under that gap budget.

## Refinable explicit S3 patch development profile

`S3_PATCH_REFINED_NODAL_PRIMAL_MORTAR_AUGMENTED_STATIC_DEV_V0` admits explicitly selected S3 V2D patches with more than two faces on either side, under declared candidate-pair and differentiation-evaluation budgets. Its contact path identity is `S3_PATCH_REFINED_AUGMENTED_PATH_DEV_V0`. Pressure coefficients remain attached to distinct **source material nodes**; contact integration subdivisions do not add coefficients. The kernel assembles support, weighted gap and pressure Gram across all face pairs. It differentiates each S3/S3 pair integral locally, then accumulates derivatives at shared pressure nodes before differentiating the projected pressure normalization. Nonoverlapping pairs remain in the declared candidate set, and exhausted budgets fail explicitly.

The local derivative route matched the earlier whole-patch residual and tangent on a smooth two-by-two case. A six-face source patch against two target faces added local pressure resolution near a penetrated corner: the coarse two-face patch had positive weighted gaps and zero pressure, while the refined patch activated its corner pressure. A public-manager directional tangent check had relative error below `1e-5`; separate tests rejected insufficient pair and differentiation budgets. The one-step nonlinear path and the [refined patch example](../examples/general_contact_refined_s3_patch.py) completed with a version-2 checkpoint. The example reported four active pressure coefficients and about −0.814 mm conservative lower gap under an explicit 5 mm development tolerance. A stricter pressure-change control with insufficient augmentation passes stopped and rolled back. Its imposed displacement drives a material corner through the target, so this example is not a tight-gap qualification or a general force-control demonstration.

Face-pair evaluation cost, conservative physical-gap bounds, mesh convergence, pressure-space stability, larger-scene search, automatic pairing and independent engineering acceptance remain open. The example's final `recovery_pair_geometry_evaluations` counts only the final contact recovery, not all trial work. The complete C0–C9 programme remains open.

An isolated wheel built from source commit `774c38a7` reproduced the refined patch example byte for byte. This verifies package inclusion and import routing for the development profile; it does not promote the profile to engineering acceptance or publication.
